Glossary: Contemporary Art- Art from the late 1970s to the present movement, created many art styles from the time usually gaining inspiration from previous/past artworks or styles. There is no single style as many styles were born and blended usually from themes such as identity and culture, social political, technology etc.  Abstract Expressionism: An art style that diverged in America around the 1940s that focus on the artists inner self as well as intrusion of the viewer.  Directional lines: A line type where it's used to direct the eye to usually a main focus point. It can literally be a line but can also uses figures or shapes to intentionally create lines.  Value Contrast: The different between the light and dark creating contrast by the different value of a color or shape.  Focal point: the main focus of an artwork
